Pork Sliders is a free iPhone game that shows ads and offers one optional
purchase that turns them off. There is no account and no sign-in, and the game
never asks for your name, your email, or anything else about you.

## What stays on your phone

The game saves your top score, the score of a round in progress, whether sound
is on or off, and whether you have bought Remove Ads, using the storage iOS
gives every app. None of it is sent to me, none of it identifies you, and
deleting the app deletes all of it.

## Ads

Ads come from a third-party ad network: a banner while you play and on the game
over screen, and a full-screen ad between the two every third time you lose.
Advertisers are responsible for their own materials and offers, and that network
may receive limited device, usage, and ad-interaction information that it
handles under its own privacy policy.

## Analytics

The game uses a third-party analytics service that records basic usage
automatically — that the app was opened, how long a session lasted, your device
model, your iOS version, and a random ID that stands for your installation
rather than for you. It collects no name, email, contacts, photos, or precise
location, and the game sends no custom events on top of that.

## The Remove Ads purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by the platform's store. All the game
learns is whether that account owns Remove Ads, which it remembers on your phone
so it knows not to load an ad the moment you open the game.

## What the game never does

There are no accounts, profiles, or notifications, and nothing you can post.
The game never touches your camera, photos, microphone, contacts, or precise
location, and nothing about you is ever sold.

## Children

Pork Sliders is not aimed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly
collect anything from them.

## Wherever you live

The law where you live may give you extra rights, such as asking for a copy of
your data or asking for it to be deleted, but almost nothing about you exists to
copy or delete. For the data the ad and analytics providers hold, their own
privacy policies explain your options; for anything else, email me and I will
help.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
